The owner of a small product does not write code. They gave a feature idea to an AI coding agent
and left. The agent wrote a design and an implementation plan, saved them as files and ended with
a chat message. When the owner comes back, they read this to do three things: understand what will
be built and how it will work for their users, make the decisions the agent left to them, and
decide whether to let the agent start building.

Write the checklist that such submissions will be graded against: the {{count}} most important
criteria a submission must meet for THIS reader to do those three things well.

Rules:
- Each item is one sentence and is checkable in a document. It must apply to any feature, not to
  a particular one.
- Substance of communication only: what the owner can learn, understand and decide, and how
  reliably. No items about visual formatting, section names, document length or following a
  particular methodology.
- No item that any document would pass trivially.
- Most important first.
- Output exactly {{count}} items as a Markdown numbered list, and nothing else.
